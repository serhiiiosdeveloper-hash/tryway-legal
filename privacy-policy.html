Privacy Policy

Tryway: Route Planner · Effective date: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Tryway app ("Tryway", "the app", "we") handles and why. The short version: Tryway has no accounts, your trips are stored on your iPhone, and we never receive your locations.

1. Who we are

Tryway is published by Serhii Kravchuk ("we", "us"). Contact: serhii.ios.developer@gmail.com.

2. Information the app uses

Location

Tryway uses your precise location to record your trips between the places you choose, to measure how long each route takes, and, if you turn on "Auto-record trips" for a route, to start and save a trip when you leave and reach the start and finish. For this feature the app asks for "Always" location access and sets up small geofences (regions) around those two places.

  • Your location, your routes, and your trips are stored only on your device.
  • We do not receive, upload, or have access to them.
  • You can turn location access off at any time in iOS Settings; trip recording will then stop working.

Places you search for and route suggestions

When you search for a place or ask for suggested ways between two points, the app uses Apple Maps services (MapKit). Your search text and the start and finish points are sent to Apple to return results. This is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data you create in the app

Route names, saved Home and Work places, trips, personal records, your display name, and your settings are stored locally on your device. If you back up your iPhone (for example with iCloud Backup), this data may be included in your backup under your own Apple account. You can export your data as a JSON file or delete it all from Settings.

Notifications

Reminders and trip notifications are scheduled on your device. We do not send push notifications from a server.

Widgets

The home screen and lock screen widget read a small summary of your routes from storage shared between the app and the widget on your device. It never leaves your device.

4. What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or email collection inside the app.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier (IDFA) collection.
  • No tracking of you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information.

5. Legal bases (EEA, UK)

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on: performance of a contract (providing the app and your subscription), your consent (location access, which you can withdraw in iOS Settings), and our legitimate interest in understanding, in an anonymous way, how the app is used and improving it.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your device: stays until you delete it. In Settings, "Delete all data" removes your routes, segments, and trips. Deleting the app removes the rest.
  • Analytics and subscription records: kept only as long as needed for the purposes above. Because they are linked to a random identifier and not to your name, we may not be able to find your records without help from you; contact us and we will do our best to delete them.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the California CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Most of your data is on your device, where you control it directly. For anything else, write to serhii.ios.developer@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. International transfers

Our analytics and subscription providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, they use safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Children

Tryway is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where that is the local age of digital consent), and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Your trip data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security (passcode, encryption). Data sent to our providers is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
